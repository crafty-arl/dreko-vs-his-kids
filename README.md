# Dreko vs His Kids

Alien dad Dreko has to get his ten kids on the school bus every morning of the school week. Miss the bus, and the day loops. He gets three tries a morning; after the third the game is over and starts from scratch. Past Friday the weeks go on and keep getting harder.

An entry for **Tiny Loop Jam 2026**. The jam window runs from Nov 20, 18:00 UTC to Nov 29, 18:00 UTC. Theme: "Small action. Big change."

## Stack

- Vite
- React
- three.js
- Deployed on Cloudflare Pages

## What is in so far

- The house as a tile map (`src/game/house.js`): Kitchen, Bathroom, Cubby room, Front hall and the Bus stop.
- Furniture in every room (`PROPS` and `SMALL` in `src/game/house.js`, pictures in `public/art/`): each thing belongs to a need of its room, such as the sink for teeth, the ten sleep cubbies, the sock basket and the shoe rack. Tall things stand against the two back walls so nothing hides a kid. `flip` turns a picture to face into the room. Since Nov 22 the things are from the 1920s (Carl: "the props need to be more 1920s"): an icebox, a cast iron range, a pedestal sink, a high-tank toilet, a school desk, satchels on hooks, and a wooden school bus.
- Lining the furniture up (Carl, Nov 23: "take another pass at the alignment of our props"): each thing in `PROPS` now says the middle of the floor it covers (`mid`), and `src/game/world.js` works out from the picture's own width where its card must stand so the picture's lowest corner touches the floor at the thing's near corner. Things against a wall are flush to it. A thing with a part on the wall (sink mirror, toilet tank, dressing table) says where its card stands (`stand`). `lean` turns a picture that was painted too front-on so its long edge runs along its wall (cubbies, pantry, shoe rack, show shelf; `card` in `src/game/art.js`). The fridge, stove, counter, cubbies and show shelf are a touch smaller so they fit side by side. So that a flat card does not cut in front of someone standing beside a wide thing, the engine copy has `back` on a thing (`src/engine/gm-three.js`): furniture takes its place in front or behind from the floor and walls behind it, and a thing out in the room (table, chairs) only by about its own width.
- Room view (`ROOM_VIEW` in `src/game/config.js`): indoors the camera frames the whole room Dreko is in and slides to the next room at a door. Outside it follows him, and the bus scene is watched from closer. The diagonal angle never changes.
- The still world around the home (`WORLD` in `src/game/config.js`, `OUTSIDE` and `SHEETS` in `src/game/house.js`): the map the engine draws is `margin` tiles bigger than the house on every side. The pavement and road run on past both ends of the house and the rest is grass. Out there stand trees, bushes, four neighbour houses, street lamps, a fire hydrant and trash cans (cut-out pictures), and a picket fence and a washing line (pictures painted from the front, stood up as real sheets so they run true along the ground). All of it is grey, nothing moves and nobody walks there. Tall things stand behind and beside the house; across the street there are only low things, because the camera looks from that side. The game keeps the house's own tile numbers; `src/game/world.js` moves every place over by the margin when it talks to the engine. The prompts are in `tools/prop-guide/world-prompts.md`.
- The ten kids (`src/game/family.js`). Each day they are dealt across the rooms by what they need.
- Dreko walks. Tap the floor, use the arrow keys or WASD, or use a controller stick or D-pad.
- One fixed diagonal camera that follows him.
- One shared walk for the whole family (`src/game/walk.js`): a step bounce, a rock, a lean, a squash on landing, a slow breath when standing, and a paper-card turn. It is code only and uses the art as it is. The **Walk test** button makes every kid pace so the walk can be checked.
- The **Map** button shows the tile map on top of the picture, for checking walls and doors.
- The screen overlay (`src/game/Overlay.jsx`): bus clock, kids on the bus, Dreko's anxiety, a bubble over each kid and the need list on Y. A kid's bubble shows their need from the start (Carl, Nov 23: the question mark and the "knowing" came out).
- The school week (`WEEK` in `src/game/config.js`): each loop is a day, Monday to Friday. A day starts when Dreko first moves and the clock runs down from 30 seconds. If the bus leaves without all ten, the same day starts over: Dreko keeps what he learned, and every kid he got on the bus buys the next try 10 more seconds. When all ten board, the next day starts with new needs and more of them (10, 12, 14, 16, then 18), so more kids need two things. After Friday the week is done. The clock waits while the need list is open. Since Nov 22 (Carl): a morning has `WEEK.tries` = 3 tries. When the bus leaves without all ten on the third, the game is over and starts again at the first Monday. After Friday the next week starts, and it is harder: `HARDER` in `src/game/config.js` and `src/game/level.js` (every kid has two needs, kids on the move every day, fewer seconds each morning down to a floor, kids a little faster each week). There is no last week.
- The title screen and the three best tries (Carl, Nov 22): `src/game/Title.jsx` shows the name, Dreko and the ten kids, a Start button (tap, A, Enter, Space or E) and the board. Dreko stands still behind it (`setHold` in `src/game/world.js`), so the bus clock cannot start. Open the page with `?notitle` to skip it. `src/game/scores.js` keeps the three best games in the browser's own storage on that device: more mornings got through is better, then more kids on the last bus, then the newer game. Nothing is sent anywhere, so every device has its own board. A game goes on the board when it is over, and the game over card shows the board with that game marked.
- How to play, Settings and Credits (Carl, Nov 22), all on the title screen. **How to play** starts a practice morning (`startTutorial` in `src/game/world.js`, `TUTOR` in `src/game/config.js`): the bus clock is stopped, seven kids are at the bus stop already, and three still need Dreko. Their four needs use all four moves, and the last kid has two needs, so the walk back from the bus stop is in it. `src/game/Tutorial.jsx` reads what is going on and says the next step (walk, walk up to a kid, press A, do the task, open the need list), lights up that kid's bubble, and ends on a card that says what the real game adds, with Start the game. **Settings** has Music and Sounds switches (`setOn` in `src/game/sound.js`, remembered on the device), a controller test and Clear for the three best tries. **Credits** is a draft for Carl to correct.
- Controller (`src/game/pad.js`, `src/game/input.js`): a browser hides a controller from a page until a button on it is pressed, so the title screen says "Controller connected" once it is seen, and Settings shows every button and stick push the game sees. On the title screen and its pages the D-pad or stick moves between buttons, A presses, B goes back, Y is How to play and Start starts. A controller whose button order the browser does not know (its `mapping` is not `standard`) can be taught in Settings: press A, B, Y, LB and RB once, and the layout is kept on that device under the controller's name (`readPad`, `saveLayout`). A D-pad sent as a hat axis is read too.
- Small things from a playtest (Nov 23): a kid made ready pops "+10s next try" out of the clock (not on the last try or in the practice morning); the "bus left" card shows who is not ready yet, with the need each one still has; a kid hops on reaching the bus stop (`BUS.hop`); the anxiety bar is labelled; the Map shows a need, a question mark or a tick over each kid; and a tap on a kid in the need list closes the list, opens the map and rings that kid there and in the house for a few seconds (`setPicked`).
- On the Odin 2 (Carl, Nov 22): `android/` is a small Android app that opens the game's page full screen in Chrome, so the handheld's own controller reaches the game (see `android/README.md`). `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json` is what lets Chrome hide its address bar for that app. `public/manifest.webmanifest` and `public/icons/` (Dreko's own picture on the paper colour) also let a phone add the game to its home screen.
- The icon set: 16 painted icons in `public/art/icons/` (ten needs, the question mark, which the game no longer uses, clock, bus, sweat drop, tick and stick). `src/game/Icon.jsx` also holds a hand-inked line version of each as a fallback.

- The mini tasks (`src/game/Task.jsx` and `src/game/tasks/`): stand next to a kid and press A (or E, Space, or the gold **Help** button). Four moves cover every need, and `MOVES` in `src/game/needs.js` says which need uses which. A miss costs time and raises Dreko's anxiety, and the clock keeps running. Each works on a controller, the keyboard and a touch screen.
  - **Hold** (breakfast, teeth): hold A and let go on the gold line.
  - **Pieces** (packed lunch, homework, show-and-tell): pieces come one at a time; pick the spot each belongs in and drop it.
  - **Trace** (permission slip, backpack): move the pen along the dotted line through every dot.
  - **Sides** (braids, socks, jacket, shoes): press left or right as each step lights up.
  - The shapes, numbers and lines inside the tasks are stand-ins until each need has its own pictures.
- Boarding: when a need is done the kid walks to the bus stop on the shared walk. A kid with nothing left waits there. A kid with one more need that day says "Oh wait, I forgot something!" at the stop, turns back and waits in the room of that need, and Dreko has to walk up again. A kid counts for the bus the moment their last need is done, wherever they are on the walk to the stop. Dreko can also catch a kid who is on the way back in and help them right there, before they reach the room.
- The bus scene (`BUS` in `src/game/config.js`): the bus pulls up at the stop for the last 10 seconds. When the morning is over the camera goes to the stop, the counted kids get on one by one and the bus drives off; when all ten are on, the driver nods first. Mom paces the far side of the street on the shared walk and stops to look at the house, so we see her front one way and her back the other; for the bus scene she comes to her spot and watches. The card comes up after the bus has gone, and it waits for **Continue** (the button, A, Enter or Space) before the same day starts over or the next day starts. The bus, the driver and Mom are pictures in `public/art/` (`bus.webp`, `driver.webp`, `mom.webp`, `mom-back.webp`); a labelled grey stand-in is drawn for any that is missing. Dreko and the kids stay off the road.
- Anxiety (`ANXIETY` in `src/game/config.js`): the bar climbs with the clock and with every miss in a mini task. It drops, with a deep breath from Dreko and a flash on the bar, for each need he gets done and each kid counted. When it is high his hands shake in the mini tasks (the gold zone of a hold shrinks, the pen of a trace wobbles, and every task trembles) and sweat drops fly off him.
- The win: all ten on the bus on Friday. The card has words for now, and **Play again** starts a new week.

- The need deal (`dealDay` in `src/game/needs.js`): there are 11 needs in the pool. Every day deals new ones at random: every kid gets one, and as the week goes on more kids get a second, always in a different room from their first. The 11th need, Shoes tied, has a line icon as a stand-in until it gets a painted one.
- Rooms (`ROOMS` in `src/game/needs.js`, `SPOTS` in `src/game/house.js`): every need belongs to a room, and a kid waits in the room of the need they have. Kitchen: breakfast, packed lunch. Bathroom: teeth, braids. Cubby room: socks, jacket, homework. Front hall: permission slip, backpack, show-and-tell, shoes. The pairing is a draft.
- Kids on the move (`ROAM` in `src/game/config.js`): on Thursday and Friday the kids walk around the room of their need instead of standing still, so Dreko has to catch one to help them. A kid stands still while their task is open.
- The rules log: the **Log** button (or `?log` in the address) lists every rule as it fires, newest first, with the day, the try and the clock: the deal, a need told, a task opened, a miss, a need done, a kid at the stop, a kid who forgot something, the bus leaving and the time earned. It is a tool for checking the game.
- Sound (`src/game/sound.js`): `menu.mp3` loops through the whole game. On top of it, the tick-tock plays for the last 10 seconds before the bus, `Bus-lose.mp3` while the "bus left" card is up, and `Bus-win.mp3` on the win. The menu music fades out under those last two and comes back after. Nothing plays until the first tap or key, as browsers ask. The **Sound** button turns it off and on. `Dad-died.mp3` has no moment in the game yet.
- The market (`src/game/Market.jsx`, numbers in `config.js` MARKET): a morning that is won pays points (5 for the morning, 1 for each try not used, 1 for each second left, at most 2 seconds, so a win pays 5 to 9 and the things cost 5 to 8: one win buys one thing, Carl, Nov 23). After the "day done" card the market opens, and points buy things for Dreko's three pockets. Points and pockets carry over from morning to morning and are gone at game over. In the house each thing is used once, when the player picks: tap it, press 1, 2 or 3, or on a controller LB moves to the next pocket and RB uses it. A thing that cannot be used right now stays in the pocket and says why.
  - Roller skates: Dreko is faster until that day is done.
  - Pocket watch: the bus clock stops for 5 seconds.
  - Whistle: every kid walking around stands still for 5 seconds.
  - Coin for the driver: once the bus is at the stop, it waits 5 more seconds.
  - Piece of candy: the kid Dreko is standing next to does their own need.
  - The five things are Carl's pick (Nov 23). Their prices, numbers and words are drafts, their icons are painted in the same pen and watercolour look as the need icons (`public/art/icons/`), and the spot for the character who runs the market is empty until Carl makes that character.
- The opening scene (Carl, Nov 23; `INTRO` in `src/game/config.js`, `startIntro` in `src/game/world.js`): Start on the title screen plays it. Mom walks up outside and says her line, Dreko stands across from her with his shoulders down, he goes into the house, and the game counts 3, 2, 1 before the bus clock starts. A, B, Start or a tap on Skip leaves it, and then the clock waits for his first step as before. Mom's line is Carl's rough wording and is a draft. It uses the pictures the game has. The ending scene with Mom is not built: it waits on Carl's words.
- Help at doors (Carl, Nov 23; `DOOR_HELP` in `config.js`): when the stick or keys push Dreko straight into a wall and an opening is a little to one side, he slides over to it. It only acts when he is stopped dead, so sliding along a wall is as before.
- Pause (Carl's card, Nov 23): the button beside Needs, P or Escape on a keyboard, Start on a controller. The whole morning stands still: the clock, Dreko and the kids. The card has Continue, and Back to the title, which ends the game in play. It also pauses by itself when the page is hidden (a phone that locks, another tab). There is no pause inside a mini task or on a card. The pause card shows the game's name, where the morning stands (day, try, bus clock, kids ready, anxiety, points, pockets), the settings and the controls.
- More settings (`src/game/Settings.jsx`, on the title's Settings page and on the pause card): music and sounds each on or off and Low, Mid or High; full screen where the browser has it; and a switch that hides the Log and Walk test buttons. All but full screen are remembered on the device.

Not in yet: each need's own task pictures, the ending screen, drawn walking frames, and sound effects (steps, task done, kid boards).
