## Prompt F: furniture for the 2.5D room (attach STYLE then CAMERA)

Picture 1 is a guide for drawing style and colour only: hand-drawn black ink outline with a soft watercolour wash on paper, in only black, white and warm grey, no other colour at all. Do not draw the thing in picture 1.

Picture 2 is a room from the 2.5D isometric game this prop is for. It shows the game's one fixed camera: the room is seen from above and turned 45 degrees, so the floor tiles are diamonds. The furniture in picture 2 looks too modern: do not copy its design, only its camera.

Draw one single prop for that 2.5D isometric game, as it would have looked in a family home in the 1920s: [THING].

Draw it from exactly the same camera as the fridge, stove and counter standing along the back wall on the right of picture 2. It stands against that same wall. Its back is toward the upper right and its front faces the lower left. Its long side runs along that wall, from the upper left down to the lower right of the picture, at the same slant as that row of furniture. On the floor it covers [FOOTPRINT]. We see its top, its front and its right end. Do not draw it front-on and do not draw it side-on.

Draw only the prop. Do not draw the room, the floor, the wall or anything else from picture 2.

The whole prop is inside the picture with clear empty space on every side. It is a plain object: no face, no eyes, no arms, no people, no characters. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border, no ground shadow. Nothing electric and nothing made of plastic.

Background: one flat pure green (#00FF00) from edge to edge. No white paper, no frame, no glow, no gradient. Nothing in the prop itself is green.

## Prompt S: a small thing or a thing that stands on its own (attach STYLE then CAMERA)

The same as Prompt F, but the fourth paragraph is: "Draw it from exactly the same camera as picture 2: from the same height, looking down at the same angle. [STANDS]"

## Prompt B: the bus (attach BUSNOW then PHOTO)

Picture 1 is the school bus in a hand-painted game: black ink outline with soft watercolour colour. Keep its painting style, its viewing angle and its direction exactly: seen from a little above in a three-quarter view, pointing toward the lower right of the picture, so we see one long side and the front, with all its wheels on the ground.

Picture 2 is a photo of a real school bus from the 1920s. Draw that kind of bus instead of the modern one in picture 1: a boxy yellow wooden body with a row of square windows framed in dark wood and a flat, slightly curved roof, on an old truck chassis with a black rounded hood, big black swooping front fenders, a radiator grille, two round brass headlamps, a running board, and yellow wooden spoked wheels with black tyres. The words SCHOOL BUS are hand-lettered in black on its side, and there is no other text.

In colour: yellow and black like the photo, in the soft watercolour of picture 1.

The bus is empty: no driver and no passengers. The driver's seat behind the windshield is empty and we can see it through the front side window.

The whole bus is inside the picture with clear empty space on every side. No border, no ground shadow, no road, no people, no museum, no chains, no posts.

Background: one flat pure green (#00FF00) from edge to edge. Nothing in the bus itself is green.
