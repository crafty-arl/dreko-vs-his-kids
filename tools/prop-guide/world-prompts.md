# Still world around the home: the 10 prompts (Nov 21, on Carl's "go, 10 pictures, 15 credits")

Model: Nano Banana 2, 1.5 credits a picture, no re-rolls. Everything black, white and warm grey.
References, from the game repo at commit f9f4859:
- STYLE = public/art/counter.webp
- CAMERA = tools/prop-guide/camera-guide.png (the kitchen from the game's own camera)
- GROUND = public/art/f-walk.jpg (a ground texture seen from straight above)

## Prompt A: things that stand up in the 2.5D world (attach STYLE then CAMERA)

Picture 1 is a guide for drawing style and colour only: hand-drawn black ink outline with a soft watercolour wash on paper, in only black, white and warm grey, no other colour at all.

Picture 2 is a room from the 2.5D isometric game this prop is for. It shows the game's one fixed camera: everything is seen from above and turned 45 degrees, so the floor tiles are diamonds.

Draw one single outdoor prop for that 2.5D isometric game: [THING].

Draw it from exactly the same camera as picture 2: from the same height, looking down at the same angle. [STANDS]

Draw only the prop. Do not draw the room, the floor, the wall, the ground, grass or anything else from picture 2.

The whole prop is inside the picture with clear empty space on every side. It is a plain object: no face, no eyes, no arms, no people, no animals, no characters. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border, no ground shadow.

Background: one flat pure green (#00FF00) from edge to edge. No white paper, no frame, no glow, no gradient. Nothing in the prop itself is green: every part of it is black, white or warm grey.

## Prompt B: flat things drawn straight from the front (attach STYLE only)

The attached picture is a guide for drawing style and colour only: hand-drawn black ink outline with a soft watercolour wash on paper, in only black, white and warm grey, no other colour at all. Do not copy its viewing angle and do not draw the thing in it.

Draw one single flat prop for a game: [THING].

Draw it straight from the front, perfectly flat, like an elevation drawing: no perspective, no slant, no top surface and no side surface showing. Level parts are perfectly level across the picture and upright parts are perfectly upright.

[DETAIL]

It is a plain object: no face, no eyes, no people, no animals, no characters. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border, no ground, no grass, no ground shadow.

Background: one flat pure green (#00FF00) from edge to edge. No white paper, no frame, no glow, no gradient. Nothing in the prop itself is green: every part of it is black, white or warm grey.

## Prompt C: the ground (attach STYLE then GROUND)

Picture 1 is a guide for drawing style and colour only: hand-drawn black ink outline with a soft watercolour wash on paper, in only black, white and warm grey, no other colour at all.

Picture 2 is a ground texture from the same game: a pavement seen from exactly straight above, filling the whole picture.

Draw a ground texture in the same way as picture 2, but of a mown grass lawn. It is seen from exactly straight above, flat, and it fills the whole picture from edge to edge with no border and no horizon. The short grass is drawn as many small ink tufts and short strokes scattered evenly over a soft warm grey wash, a little darker than the pavement in picture 2, with a few faintly darker patches. Only black, white and warm grey: the grass is grey, never green.

It is even all over: no path, no flowers, no stones, no leaves, no objects, no shadows, no big shapes, no dark corners, nothing that would show as a pattern when the picture is repeated side by side like tiles. No text, no letters, no border.
